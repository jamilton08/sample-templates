What math taught me beyond the numbers

When I started, I thought math was about getting the one right answer. By the end, I understood it's really about building an argument — every step has to be justified, and being able to explain my reasoning is more valuable than the answer itself.

This connects directly to being career ready: in any job, I'll need to look at data, spot patterns, and make a decision I can defend. The statistics project especially showed me how numbers can be honest or misleading depending on how you present them.

For college, I now have the persistence to sit with a hard problem instead of giving up — a skill I'll need in every subject, not just math.
