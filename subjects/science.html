How science changed the way I think

Science taught me to separate what I think is true from what the evidence actually shows. Designing experiments forced me to control variables and admit when my hypothesis was wrong — which turned out to be the most useful lesson of all.

For my career, this is the core of working with data and making evidence-based claims. Whatever field I go into, I'll need to test ideas instead of assuming.

As a global citizen, understanding the scientific method helps me evaluate claims about climate, health, and technology instead of believing whatever I read first.
